The Geography

Every coast within a hundred miles.

2961 Lakeview Drive sits almost exactly at the centre of the Florida peninsula. From the dock, the Gulf is sixty‑six miles west and the Atlantic sixty‑six miles east; Tampa, Orlando, Sarasota, Fort Myers and Melbourne all fall inside seventy‑six. That single fact sets the footprint. The buyer for a ten‑thousand‑square‑foot lake estate in Highlands County is very likely already a Floridian — someone who knows the ridge, keeps a boat, and can be standing on this dock inside two hours of leaving home. So the mail goes to Florida addresses, ranked by proximity and capacity. The northern feeder cities are the digital campaign’s job; mail is delivered where people actually receive it.

The Footprint

Four audiences, one drop.

The four tiers are not four sizes of the same list. Two of them are asked to produce a buyer. The other two are asked to produce a conversation — the neighbour who mentions it at the club, the Sebring family whose cousin in Tampa has been looking for water. They are filtered differently because they are doing different work, and they are reported separately so that the difference is visible afterwards.

Why Five Hundred, And Where It Was Cut
Five hundred pieces in a single drop is the Aperture standard, and five hundred at this price point only works if the geography is cut rather than thinned. So Naples, Palm Beach and Boca Raton come out altogether — each over ninety miles out, each priced above this house — and the pieces go instead into the shoreline and the ridge, where the referral argument is strongest, and into a short list of comparably priced households inside a two‑hour drive. The count and the split are both set.

The Ledger

The footprint, zip by zip.

Written out so that it can be executed. Each tier is a separate pull with its own screen; all four are then deduplicated against one another before any cap is applied, so no household receives two pieces and no zip is counted twice. Where a zip appears in more than one tier — Sebring sits in both the shoreline radius and the county tier, Lake Wales in both the county and the Lakeland cluster — the narrower tier takes the record.

The band runs from roughly six tenths to one and six tenths of the asking price. An open threshold of a million dollars and above would look generous and behave badly: it pulls in every coastal estate far above this one, households that will never trade down into a Highlands County address. Bracketing the price keeps the list to people for whom this is a lateral move or a modest step up — and it is the single change that most improves response on a mailing this small.

Appends On Every Record
Occupation detail, property type, income rank, purchase amount range and length of residence, so response can be segmented after the fact rather than guessed at. Add a tier code at export; it is what makes per‑market reporting possible at all.
The Ranking Is The Decision
These pulls over‑return heavily: one affluent Florida zip will commonly hand back two thousand records against a target of a hundred and fifty. The thresholds define the pool; the sort selects the list. It is specified above and should not be left to the operator.

What Happens Next

Mail fewer, mail better.

The strategy is settled: five hundred pieces, four tiers, the zip lists on page 04 and the screens and ranking on page 06. What follows is execution, in this order. One item is genuinely outstanding, and it is a scheduling matter rather than a question of approach.

The Footprint
Four tiers across central and coastal Florida, chosen on drive time and on comparable value. Naples, Palm Beach and Boca Raton are left out deliberately.
The List
Pulled tier by tier to the specification on page 06, deduplicated across the four tiers, ranked and capped at 500.
The Piece
A 6 × 10.5 in oversized card, double sided. Chosen because it is read without being opened, and because the photograph carries the house at that size.
The Drop Date
Still to be set, against print and list lead times, inside the campaign window. A single drop.
