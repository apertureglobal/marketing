The Market

One television market, ten counties wide.

2961 Lakeview Drive stands in Highlands County, on the Lake Wales Ridge at the centre of the Florida peninsula. For every kind of advertising that is bought by market rather than by address, that places the house in the Tampa–St. Petersburg (Sarasota) market — the eleventh largest in the country, and ten counties across. Highlands is one of those ten. The other nine are drawn darker on the map opposite, and they are where the roadside screens belong: Hillsborough, Pinellas, Polk, Pasco, Hernando, Citrus, Manatee, Sarasota and Hardee.

The paler counties are the second ring — Orlando and its suburbs, the ridge and lake country to the north, and the interior south. They matter because none of them is far from this dock, but they sit outside the market this campaign is bought in, and they are reached by the online advertising instead.

The Argument

The road is the only place this house is unavoidable.

A ten‑thousand‑square‑foot estate on two hundred and fifty feet of Lake Jackson is not a house people search for. Nobody types ‘Sebring’ into a property portal hoping for this. The buyer is somewhere on the Gulf coast or in the Orlando suburbs, is not looking inland, and has to be told that fresh water at this scale exists less than seventy miles inland. Print and portals wait to be opened. A screen over a commuter road does not wait.

Reach the market, not just the postcode
The targeting favours the parts of the market that send people inland towards the ridge, rather than only the area immediately around the house. Sebring itself is covered, but the people already in Sebring are mainly the direct mail campaign’s job.
02

The Targeting

Chosen by area, not by billboard.

It is worth being clear about how these screens are bought, because it is not how billboards used to work. We do not rent one particular sign on one particular road for a month. We buy digital screens programmatically — we tell the exchange which parts of the market to serve the house into, and it places the advertisement on whatever digital screens are available in those areas while the campaign runs. So there is no list of board addresses in this document, and there will not be one. What we choose, and what this document sets out, is the geography.

In practice that means roadside and public digital screens across the ten counties on the previous page, weighted towards the five areas below.

Where The Emphasis Sits
Areas 01 and 02 take the largest share of delivery, because they are where a buyer for a lake estate on the ridge is most likely to already be. Tampa, St. Petersburg and the Sarasota coast take the next largest share, for reach and for standing. The remaining counties run at a lower, steady share so that nowhere in the market is dark.
We would rather run everywhere in the market for the full thirty days at a measured weight than crowd two weeks and then disappear. A roadside frame does its work by being there again on Thursday.
One consequence of buying this way is worth saying plainly: we cannot promise you a photograph of your house on a named sign at a named junction. What we can say is which parts of Florida saw it, and how often, once the month is over.

What Happens Next

The month ahead, in order.

The market, the targeted areas and the emphasis are settled — that is the work in the pages before this one, and it is the judgement you are paying us for. What remains is the sequence, and two figures that do not exist yet. Where a number is missing below it is missing on purpose: an estimate offered now would be a guess wearing a suit, and this house is not marketed on guesses.

Now
The advertisement is built from the photography of the house and the lake frontage, in the Aperture setting used on the cards and online. You will see it and approve it before a single screen carries it.
21 September
The campaign goes live across the ten counties of the Tampa–St. Petersburg (Sarasota) market, weighted into the five areas set out on the previous page.
Through The Month
Steady delivery for the full thirty days rather than two crowded weeks. Polk and Highlands carry the largest share throughout, Tampa, St. Petersburg and the Sarasota coast the next, and no part of the market goes dark.
21 October
The campaign closes. A report follows, covering where in Florida the house was shown and for how long, with whatever the exchange and its operators are able to verify.
