The Approach

Precision over volume.

Your home is not advertised everywhere. It is advertised where it matters. Fifteen acres on Lake Mills, with four stalls, four paddocks, a sand arena and a hundred feet of dock, is not bought by whoever happens to be looking for a large house near Orlando. It is bought by someone who keeps horses, or intends to, and who will not give up the water or the half-hour drive into the city to get the land. That is a small group of people, and we know where they live.

Where It Runs

The right buyers live in the right places.

A working barn changes who the buyer is. Most of them are already in Florida, which is why the largest share of the campaign stays here — leaning on Central Florida, on Ocala, and on Wellington, where the winter riding season fills every stable in Palm Beach County. Beyond Florida we go to the five northern places that send those same families south.
