The Approach

Precision over volume.

Your home is not advertised everywhere. It is advertised where it matters. A Mediterranean estate of ten thousand square feet, with two hundred and fifty feet of frontage on Lake Jackson, a dock of its own and garaging for ten cars, is bought by a particular kind of buyer. Many of them are already in Florida. Others are in the northern cities that have sent families to Florida water for generations. The campaign therefore runs in two parts, one inside the state and one beyond it, so each audience is approached on its own terms.
