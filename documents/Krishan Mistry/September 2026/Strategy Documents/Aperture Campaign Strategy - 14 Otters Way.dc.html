The Approach

The space is here. So is the fast train.

Your home will not be advertised everywhere. It will be advertised where it matters. The campaign is built in two parts so each is approached on its own terms.

Where It Runs

Every buyer for this house is on one railway line.

The five markets are not scattered. They sit in a single line running south from Peterborough to King’s Cross, and that line is the argument for this house. The greater part of the campaign stays close to home — Peterborough and its villages, then Huntingdon and St Neots, then Cambridge and South Cambridgeshire. A family already in the county knows what £1,050,000 buys here, and knows that 2,793 sq ft with three receptions and a garden of this size is not the usual offer. The remaining effort goes to London and to the commuter towns on the same line, where the same money buys a fraction of the space. A national footprint would be the wrong shape for this home, and it is not used.
