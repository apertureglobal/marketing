Who This Is For

Not a trophy. A trade‑up.

At $1,799,000 this is an established‑neighbourhood family house, and the list has to be built that way. A luxury pull calibrated for seven‑figure‑plus trophy stock would return Belle Meade estates and Williamson County acreage — households who are already past this house, not moving into it. The buyer here is coming the other way: out of a smaller house on a smaller lot somewhere on the west side, into 1.15 acres, five bedrooms and a renovation that is already finished.

Williamson County, Handled Deliberately
Brentwood and Franklin are nine and thirteen miles out and are not in Davidson County at all — they are Williamson. They are also the single largest pool of households in Middle Tennessee at this price, which is exactly why they are left out rather than forgotten. A Brentwood household that wanted an acre already bought one, thirty minutes further from the city. This property’s argument is the reverse: the land is here, on the west side, eight miles in. Mailing Williamson would put the list to work arguing against itself.

The Footprint

Four tiers, one drop.

Each tier is asked to do one job, and only one. Tier 01 is asked to start conversations on your own street. Tier 02 carries the weight of the campaign — the west‑side household in a smaller house who has been looking for land inside the county. Tier 03 is the lateral move at the same money. Tier 04 is the narrowest and most specific bet: households already at this price on a quarter acre, for whom the land is the entire upgrade.

Why Five Hundred Works Here, And Where It Was Cut
Five hundred pieces in a single drop is the Aperture standard. At this price it is a workable number rather than a punishing one — but only because the geography is cut rather than thinned. Williamson County comes out whole. So does everything east of the river and everything south of Oak Hill. What is left is a six‑zip west‑side corridor where tenure, lot size and value bands can each do real work, and where 38 per cent of the delivery sits on the one audience most likely to move.

The floor logic is where a trade‑up listing parts company with a trophy one. On a seven‑figure‑plus trophy asset a 0.6× floor is still an exclusive number and screens to genuine peers. Apply 0.6× here and it lands at $1,079,400 — which sits above the current home of the exact household this property is for, and quietly deletes the primary audience. So Band A drops to 0.45×. Its ceiling comes down too, to 1.10×: a household already in a $2.5M house is not trading up into this one, and belongs in Band B or nowhere.

Why Both Bands Are Bracketed Rather Than Floored
An open threshold — every household at $1,000,000 and above — looks generous and behaves badly. It returns the whole top of Middle Tennessee, most of which is already past a 3,800‑square‑foot house in West Meade and will never trade down into one. Bracketing at both ends is what keeps the list to households for whom this is a step up or a step across, and it is why the four caps can be met without widening the map.
Applied To The Purchase Tiers
Owner‑occupied only. Single family and condominium only. Household income above $200,000 on Tier 02 and above $250,000 — the top band available — on Tiers 03 and 04. The top band alone is a trophy screen and would cut qualified trade‑up households at this price.
Length Of Residence
Seven years and over on Tier 02, five and over on Tier 03, eight and over on Tier 04. Tenure is the closest thing on a mailing list to a household having outgrown its house, and on this listing it does more work than income does.

Suppression — Applied Before Any Cap
Suppress 805 Cammack Court and every adjoining parcel, so this household does not receive its own mailing — the subject property sits inside the Tier 01 radius by definition. Suppress the listing agent’s own address. Suppress the Aperture Global office address. Then deduplicate the four tiers against one another, and only then apply the caps.
Where The Tiers Overlap
37205 appears in Tiers 01, 02 and 03. Where a household qualifies twice, the narrower tier takes the record and the wider tier gives it up. 37221 crosses the county line at its western edge — it is held to Davidson carrier routes only.
The Ranking Is The Decision
These pulls over‑return heavily — one west‑side zip will commonly hand back close to two thousand records against a target under two hundred. The thresholds define the pool; the sort selects the list, and it is not left to the operator.
Appends On Every Record
Occupation detail, property type, income rank, purchase amount range and length of residence, so response can be segmented rather than guessed at. A tier code is written at export; it is what makes per‑market reporting possible.

What Happens Next

Mail fewer, mail better.

The strategy is settled: five hundred pieces, four tiers, one drop, weighted onto the west‑side households most likely to trade up into an acre. What follows is the order of work, and the one item that is a matter of scheduling rather than judgement.

The Footprint
Set. Four tiers, weighted 100 / 190 / 140 / 70, with 38 per cent of the delivery on the trade‑up audience. Six zips, all held to Davidson County carrier routes. Williamson County, the east side and everything beyond eight miles are excluded.
The Bands
Set. Derived from $1,799,000 — Band A at 0.45× to 1.10×, $800,000 to $1,975,000; Band B at 0.80× to 1.60×, $1,450,000 to $2,875,000. If the price moves, the bands are re‑derived from the new figure rather than rescaled.
The List
Pulled to the specification on the previous page. Suppressed against 805 Cammack Court and every adjoining parcel, and against the agent and office addresses; deduplicated across all four tiers; then capped by ranking, not by widening. Tier codes travel with the export so response is read market by market.
The Piece
A 6 × 9 in card. What sells this house is the land and the finish — the park‑like lot at the closed end of the street, the screened porch, and the 2022 kitchen with its oversized island. That wants one wide exterior and a tight interior set, not a gallery. You will see the artwork and approve it before it runs.

Open only because it is scheduling. Fixed against print and list lead times, and confirmed to you once the pull returns.
