Who This Is For

Lot size sorts this list better than price does.

Two and a third million buys a great many houses in Davidson County. It buys very few that come with two level acres, a gunite pool, brick terraces, a pool house and no association to answer to. A list sorted on assessed value alone is therefore sorted on the wrong axis: it finds households who can afford the house without telling us whether they want what the house is. Land is the variable, and the mailing runs down two paths because of it.

Path One
They already hold land and will not give it up.
Owners on three quarters of an acre and up, across Forest Hills, Oak Hill, Green Hills, Belle Meade, Hillwood and West Meade
They read acreage correctly on sight, because they live on it already
Moving means matching the land, not just the house — which is exactly the short list this property sits on
A level lot with an existing pool and terraces removes the work they know they would otherwise take on
Path Two
They have the house and none of the ground.
Owners on a third of an acre or less in 12South, Belmont‑Hillsboro, Berry Hill, Sylvan Park and East Nashville
They have already bought at this price point, on a lot a fraction of this size
The upgrade they cannot buy in place is ground — and leaving the county is the usual price of getting it
Seven miles to the core keeps the commute and the schools they moved for intact
Both Paths Converge
The two paths are screened on opposite sides of the same measurement. Path one qualifies on a large lot, path two on a small one, and nothing else in the stack changes. That single inversion is what lets five hundred pieces cover both the households who understand the asset and the households shopping for exactly what it offers.
A third audience is mailed on neither test. The immediate neighbourhood receives the card unscreened, because its job is referral rather than purchase, and a value filter only removes the neighbours who talk.

The Allocation

Five hundred pieces, four jobs.

The drop is one mailing, weighted across four tiers. Weighting is a decision, made here and made on purpose: the two qualifying paths carry the majority of the delivery between them, the neighbourhood tier is kept deliberately small because it is a referral instrument, and a fourth tier reaches south‑side households who already own ground and would be stepping up rather than across.

Qualification

Banded on the asking price, bracketed on the land.

Every threshold below is arithmetic on two published figures and nothing else: the asking price of two million three hundred thousand dollars, and the lot at one point nine seven acres. An open floor — anything above a million, anything above an acre — drags in estates far above this house that will never trade down into it. So both screens are bracketed at each end.

Three quarters of an acre is the point at which a Davidson County household is buying ground rather than a garden, so it reads two acres as more of what it already has. A third of an acre is small enough that the gap is the whole proposition. Between the two sits a middle that responds to neither argument, and five hundred pieces cannot carry it.
Household income is set above two hundred and fifty thousand dollars across every filtered tier — the top band the data provider offers — with property type limited to single family and condominium and residence restricted to owner‑occupied throughout. If the asking price moves, these bands are re‑derived from the new figure rather than rescaled.

Suppression — Applied To Every Tier
Suppress 806 Willowdale Ct and every adjoining parcel; the listing agent’s own residential address; the Aperture Global office address; and all records flagged vacant, deceased or undeliverable. Without this the seller receives their own postcard — the subject property sits inside the tier 01 radius by definition.
Deduplication & Geographic Exclusion
The four lists are deduplicated against one another in tier order, 01 first, so a household appearing in two tiers is mailed once. Zip 37220 spans tiers 01, 02 and 04 and is the principal overlap. All Williamson County records are excluded, including any 37027 record that surfaces through a radius pull.
Appended For Segmentation
Occupation detail · property type · lot size · income rank · purchase amount range · length of residence. Appended on every record so response can be segmented by tier and by market after the drop rather than reported as a single undifferentiated figure.
Pulls at this specification over‑return by a wide margin, which is the intended behaviour. The cap does the final work, and the ranking decides which households survive it.

What Happens Next

Mail fewer, mail better.

The footprint, the tiering and the filters above are set. What follows is sequence — the order in which the drop is executed inside the campaign window, and the two figures that are genuinely not yet fixed.

01 The four tier pulls are run to the specification on the previous page, ranked, capped and deduplicated in tier order.
02 Suppression is applied last and checked by hand against the subject address and its adjoining parcels before the file is released.
03 Card artwork is produced from the property photography and sent to you for approval. Nothing goes to print until you have seen it.
04 The single drop is scheduled inside the thirty‑day campaign window, set against print and list lead times.
05 Response is logged against the appended tier and market fields, so enquiry can be read back to the neighbourhoods that produced it.
